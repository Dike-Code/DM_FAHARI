Who we place

Five roles, one standard of care.

CNA

Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)

The hands and heart of every facility. Our CNAs are state-certified, CPR current, and trained in dignity-first resident care — bathing, transfers, vitals, documentation, and the small kindnesses that make a long shift bearable.

  • State certification verified
  • BLS / CPR current
  • TB, drug screen, background check cleared
  • Hoyer lift & transfer trained
LPN / LVN

Licensed Practical / Vocational Nurse (LPN / LVN)

Medication administration, wound care, IV therapy where scope allows, and clinical leadership on the floor. Our LPNs and LVNs bridge CNA care and RN oversight — the steady backbone of every shift we staff. Title varies by state; credentials are equal.

  • Active state LPN / LVN license
  • Med-pass & G-tube competency
  • IV certification (where state permits)
  • Minimum 1 year clinical experience
RN

Registered Nurse (RN)

Charge nurses, ICU, ER, med-surg, telemetry, OR, and L&D. Compact license holders ready for multi-state travel. We credential to your facility's exact specs before the start date — no day-one surprises.

  • Active RN license (compact preferred)
  • ACLS / PALS / NRP as specialty requires
  • Specialty certs verified (CCRN, CEN, RNC)
  • Skills checklist & references on file
MED TECH

Medication Technician (Med Tech)

Trained medication aides who assist with medication administration under nurse supervision. A critical bridge role in assisted living, group homes, and long-term care where med-pass volume is high.

  • State medication aide certification
  • Supervised med-pass competency verified
  • Error reporting & documentation trained
  • CPR / BLS current
CAREGIVER

Caregiver / Home Health Aide (HHA)

Compassionate support for activities of daily living — bathing, dressing, meal prep, mobility assistance, and companionship. Placed across home care, assisted living, group homes, and memory care settings.

  • HHA certification or equivalent training
  • Background check & reference verified
  • CPR / First Aid current
  • Dementia & behavioral care experience

Our screening process

Six checkpoints between application and your floor.

We reject the majority of applicants we interview. That's how the nurses who do make it onto your unit are the right ones.

  1. 01

    Application & phone screen

    30-minute structured interview, scope verification, and shift preference mapping.

  2. 02

    License & credential audit

    Primary-source verification of every license, certification, and continuing-education hour.

  3. 03

    Background & OIG check

    7-year criminal, OIG/GSA exclusion list, sex offender registry, and motor vehicle record.

  4. 04

    Health & drug screen

    10-panel drug, TB (2-step or QFT), titers (MMR/Varicella/HepB), COVID and flu per facility policy.

  5. 05

    Skills checklist & clinical reference

    Specialty-specific competency self-assessment plus two verified clinical references from the last 24 months.

  6. 06

    Facility-specific orientation

    EMR training, facility policy review, and a paired first shift whenever the contract allows.
